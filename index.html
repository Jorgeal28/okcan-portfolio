Nacho is a Spanish designer from Valencia with a strong background and a deep passion for creativity. He graduated in Industrial Design from the Polytechnic University of Valencia (UPV), where he also spent a year studying at Middlesex University in London. After completing an internship as an industrial designer, he decided to focus his career on the automotive and creative fields, pursuing a Master’s degree in Styling at the UPV. His master’s final project was developed at Cupra (part of the Volkswagen Group), an experience that reinforced his connection to automotive design.

Since then, he has worked both as an industrial designer and as a freelance creative, covering the entire design process; from the conception of an idea to its realization. He has contributed to projects ranging from exterior car design, bodykits and rims to restyling military vehicles, showcasing versatility and technical expertise.

Nacho’s professional journey has allowed him to refine his skills and approach each project with passion and precision, delivering innovative ideas that balance aesthetics with functionality while pushing creative boundaries.
